Common questions

Do I need an account?
No. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone.
How are recipes suggested?
With Gemini, Google's AI, only after you allow it. The app checks every recipe against your pantry, allergies and diet before showing it.
Where do the calories come from?
They are worked out in the app from the TACO food table (UNICAMP) and are always estimates. Despensa doesn't replace advice from your nutritionist or doctor.
How do I turn Gemini off?
In Profile › Privacy and AI. Without it your pantry still works, but suggestions and reading photos and plans are off.
How do I delete my data?
Deleting the app removes everything from your device. The app doesn't keep your data on its own servers.
