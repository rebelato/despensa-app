Privacy Policy

Despensa – What to Cook · Last updated: October 21, 2026 (anonymous usage)

Despensa suggests recipes from what you have at home. This policy explains what data the app uses, where it stays and who it is shared with.

What stays only on your iPhone

Your profile (diet, goals, allergies, dislikes, seasonings and basics, and your nutritionist's plan), your pantry, the week's menu, saved recipes and the history of what you cooked, with notes and photos. This data is stored on your device and doesn't go to Despensa's servers. The app has no servers of its own and no user accounts.

What is sent to Gemini (Google)

Some features use Gemini, Google's AI, through Firebase AI Logic. Nothing is sent before you allow it; the app asks on first launch, and you can change it anytime in Profile › Privacy and AI. With your permission, only the data each request needs is sent:

  • Recipe suggestions and the week's menu: your pantry list and your profile (diet, allergies, dislikes, goals and parts of your nutritionist's plan).
  • Photo of your fridge or receipt: the photo, to recognize the foods.
  • Nutritionist's plan: the photo or PDF of the plan, to read the meals, and the items of the meals, to estimate their calories.
  • Voice in the pantry: the text of what you said. Speech recognition runs on the device when it can; the audio isn't sent to Gemini.
  • Ask a question: the recipe, your question, your pantry and your profile.
  • Reading aloud while cooking: the text of the steps and answers.

This data is used only to answer the request. Google may keep it for a limited time under the Gemini API terms and the Google Privacy Policy.

Anonymous usage

Despensa uses Google Analytics for Firebase to learn which screens and features are used and what the Gemini calls cost (for example: "opened a recipe", "made the week's menu", how many foods the pantry has, in ranges). Food names, recipes, diet, allergies, the plan, photos and what you type or say are never sent. The app uses the version without the advertising identifier (IDFA), with ad signals turned off, and doesn't link this data to your identity. You can turn it off in Profile › Privacy and AI › Share anonymous usage.

Protecting access

Firebase App Check (Google) uses Apple's App Attest to confirm requests come from the genuine app. It uses a device attestation that doesn't identify you.

What Despensa doesn't do

  • It doesn't sell your data or use it for ads.
  • It doesn't track you across apps and websites.
  • It has no ads or in-app purchases.

iPhone permissions

Camera and photos, to snap your fridge, a receipt, your plan or the dish you made. Microphone and speech recognition, to say what you have and to use voice commands while cooking. You can change them in the iPhone's Settings.

Health

Calories and macros are estimates worked out in the app from the TACO food table (UNICAMP). Despensa doesn't offer diagnosis or treatment and doesn't replace advice from health professionals.

Your rights and deleting your data

You can edit or delete your data in the app, and deleting the app removes everything from your device. For questions about your data, write to despensa.suporte@outlook.com.

Children

Despensa isn't directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with its date.
